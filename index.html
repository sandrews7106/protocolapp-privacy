Application Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how applications developed and maintained by XpressoMedic handle user information.

Applications Covered

  • SCEMS Clinical Guidelines (SCEMS Protocols)
  • XpressoMedic Scenarios
  • Ventilator Strategy

Information Collection

XpressoMedic applications do not require user registration, login credentials, or cloud-based user accounts. The applications are not designed to collect personally identifiable information or protected health information.

Local Data and Storage

Application content, preferences, settings, and information entered into interactive tools may be processed or stored locally on the user's device.

Users should not enter personally identifiable patient information into these applications.

Data Sharing

XpressoMedic does not sell personal information. Information entered into application tools is not automatically transmitted to XpressoMedic.

Certain applications may provide optional sharing features. Information shared through these features is transmitted only through user-initiated actions using the selected messaging, email, or sharing service.

Third-Party Services

XpressoMedic applications do not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking services. Links to external websites and services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Intended Use

SCEMS Clinical Guidelines: Provides access to clinical guidelines and reference materials for Sullivan County EMS personnel and affiliated providers.

XpressoMedic Scenarios: Provides interactive clinical scenarios for healthcare education and training.

Ventilator Strategy: Provides interactive, physiology-based education focused on mechanical ventilation and respiratory mechanics.

XpressoMedic Scenarios and Ventilator Strategy are intended exclusively for educational purposes and are not designed for direct patient care, diagnosis, treatment, or patient-specific medical decision-making.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as applications and their features evolve. Updates will be published on this webpage with a revised effective date.
